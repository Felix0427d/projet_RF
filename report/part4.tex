
\section{Antenna Design}

This section presents the design and simulation of an antenna
operating in the 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi frequency band. The antenna
is designed and optimized using \href{https://www.emerge-software.com}
{EMerge}, a 3D electromagnetic simulation software.

The objective is to achieve a compromise between antenna gain,
bandwidth, radiation efficiency, and physical dimensions.

The target specifications are summarized in
Table~\ref{tab:antenna-specifications}.

\begin{table}[htbp]
    \centering
    \caption{Target antenna specifications}
    \label{tab:antenna-specifications}
    \begin{tabular}{|l|l|}
        \hline
        \textbf{Specification} & \textbf{Target value} \\
        \hline
        Frequency band & 2.400--2.4835 GHz \\
        \hline
        Center frequency & 2.45 GHz \\
        \hline
        Antenna type & Microstrip patch / patch array \\
        \hline
        Substrate material & FR-4 (initial choice) \\
        \hline
        Relative permittivity & $\varepsilon_r \approx 4.4$ \\
        \hline
        Substrate thickness & 1.6 mm \\
        \hline
        Input impedance & 50 $\Omega$ \\
        \hline
        Polarization & Linear \\
        \hline
        Impedance bandwidth & $\geq 83.5$ MHz \\
        \hline
        Return loss ($S_{11}$) & $\leq -10$ dB \\
        \hline
        VSWR & $\leq 2$ \\
        \hline
        Realized gain (single patch) & $\geq 5$ dBi \\
        \hline
        Realized gain (2$\times$2 array) & $\geq 9$ dBi \\
        \hline
        Radiation efficiency & $\geq 70\%$ \\
        \hline
        Radiation pattern & Directional (broadside) \\
        \hline
        Feeding technique & 50 $\Omega$ microstrip line \\
        \hline
        Antenna dimensions & To be optimized \\
        \hline
    \end{tabular}
\end{table}
